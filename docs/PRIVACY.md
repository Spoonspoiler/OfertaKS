# Privacy

OfertaKS should collect as little personal data as possible.

Current app:

- no accounts
- no analytics
- no advertising SDK
- no contacts permission
- no microphone permission
- no location permission yet
- pantry, basket, favorites, and preferences are local

Future community features:

- do not require public social profiles
- do not expose reporter location history
- sync only observation data needed for product/merchant usefulness
- attach provenance and confidence to community data
- let the mobile app remain useful when community sync is offline
